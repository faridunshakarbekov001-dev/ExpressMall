Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products, shipping, returns, and more.

📦 Shipping & Delivery

How long does shipping take? ⌄

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping is available for 2-3 business day delivery.

Do you ship internationally? ⌄

Yes, we ship to over 100 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and times vary by location.

Can I track my order? ⌄

Absolutely! You'll receive a tracking number via email as soon as your order ships.

What are shipping costs? ⌄

Shipping costs vary based on location and item weight. Orders over $50 qualify for free shipping to continental US addresses.

↩️ Returns & Refunds

What's your return policy? ⌄

We offer a 30-day return policy on most items. Products must be in original condition with all packaging and accessories.

How do I initiate a return? ⌄

Contact our support team with your order number. We'll provide you with a prepaid return label and further instructions.

How long for refunds to process? ⌄

Once we receive and verify your return, refunds are processed within 5-10 business days.

Can I exchange items instead? ⌄

Yes! We offer free exchanges within 30 days. Contact support to arrange your exchange.

🔒 Payment & Security

Is my payment information secure? ⌄

Absolutely! We use industry-standard SSL encryption and PCI DSS compliance to protect your payment data.

What payment methods do you accept? ⌄

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfers.

Can I save my payment info? ⌄

Yes, you can securely save payment information to your account for faster checkout on future purchases.

Do you offer financing options? ⌄

Yes, we offer installment payment plans through our financial partners for purchases over $100.

👤 Accounts & Orders

How do I create an account? ⌄

Click on "Sign Up" at the top of the page and enter your email address and a password. You can also sign up with Google or Apple ID.

Can I guest checkout? ⌄

Yes, you can check out as a guest. However, creating an account helps you track orders and save preferences.

How do I view my orders? ⌄

Log into your account and go to "Order History" to see all your current and past orders.

Can I modify my order? ⌄

Orders can only be modified within 1 hour of purchase. Contact support immediately if you need to make changes.

Still have questions?

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Our team is here to help!
